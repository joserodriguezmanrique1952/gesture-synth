## 🚀 Running gesture-synth for the First Time

When you double-click the application icon, gesture-synth will open a small window showing your webcam feed. You will see a grid or piano-style layout overlaid on the video. That is your instrument.

Follow these steps to play your first chord:

1. **Allow camera access** - If Windows asks for permission to use your camera, click **"Allow"**.
2. **Position yourself** - Sit about 2 to 3 feet away from the camera. Make sure your entire upper body and both hands are visible.
3. **Raise your hand** - Hold one hand up, palm facing the camera. You should see a stick figure or dots tracking your hand joints.
4. **Move your hand** - When your fingertips touch a highlighted zone on the screen, you will hear a chord play. Move slowly to explore different sounds.
5. **Use both hands** - Bring your other hand into view to trigger additional notes or layers, creating richer harmonies.

You are now playing music with your gestures. Practice moving your hands left, right, up, and down to discover all the sounds available.

## 🎮 Understanding the Controls

gesture-synth maps your hand movements to musical chords in an intuitive way:

- **Vertical movement** changes the pitch or chord type (major, minor, etc.)
- **Horizontal movement** switches between different chord groups
- **Distance from the camera** controls volume - move closer to make it louder, move back to make it softer
- **Making a fist** triggers a percussive or staccato sound
- **Spreading your fingers** sustains the sound longer

The visual interface on your screen shows exactly which zone you are activating. The colors change as you move, giving you clear feedback about what note or chord is about to play.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

### 🎥 Camera Not Detected

If you see a black screen or an error message about your camera:

- Ensure no other application (like Zoom or your browser) is using the camera at the same time
- Close those applications and restart gesture-synth
- Plug your USB webcam directly into your computer instead of a hub or extension cable

### 🖐️ Hand Not Being Tracked

If the hand dots are not appearing even with your hand in front of the camera:

- Make sure your background is well-lit - avoid strong backlight
- Ensure your hand is not too close to the camera lens (minimum 12 inches)
- Reduce other moving objects in the camera frame for better focus

### 🔊 No Sound

If you cannot hear audio:

- Check that your computer volume is turned up and not muted
- Plug in headphones or external speakers if your built-in speakers are faulty
- In gesture-synth's settings menu (the gear icon), confirm the output device is set to your default speaker

### 🐌 Slow Performance

If the application feels laggy or the dots move slowly:

- Close other demanding programs like games, video editors, or many browser tabs
- Reduce the resolution by opening the settings panel and selecting a lower camera quality
- Make sure your laptop is plugged into power, as battery-saving mode reduces performance

## ✨ Tips for Making Beautiful Music

- **Start slow** - Move your hand gradually. Sudden jumps can produce harsh sounds.
- **Use flowing motions** - Imagine your hand is a paintbrush painting music in the air. Smooth movements create melodic phrases.
- **Layer sounds with two hands** - Hold one hand steady to play a drone chord while moving the other hand to add melody.
- **Experiment with rhythm** - Open and close your fist in patterns to add a beat to your performance.
- **Record your session** - Use your computer's built-in recording tool (Windows Game Bar with Win+G) to capture your musical ideas.
